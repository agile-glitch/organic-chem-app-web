How accurate is the reaction engine? measured against recorded reactions, not claimed

Every one of the 14,489 recorded reactions is offered to the engine in every sensible way — each ordered pair of reactants through the molecule + molecule path, and the largest reactant with every taught reagent the recorded agents map to. One strict question is asked, by canonical structure: is the engine's major product the product that was actually isolated? Records whose recorded product is a solvent or a fragment of a much larger reactant are counted as data errors, not misses. The run takes a few minutes in the browser and can be stopped and resumed; the result is kept.
